Introduction

Keeping a lab notebook helps track experiments, observations, and results accurately

Materials needed

  • Notebook (spiral or bound)
  • Pen (blue or black ink)
  • Ruler (optional)
  • Labels or sticky notes

Steps

  1. Title Your Experiment

-Write the experiment title at the top of the page. -Include the date and your name.

  1. Record purpose & Hypothesis
  • Clearly state the purpose of the experiment.
  • Write your hypothesis
  1. Document Methods
  • Record each step of your experiment.
  • Ne detailed enough so someone else could replicate it.

4.** Observation & Data**

  • Record all observations and measurements.
  • Use tables or drawings if needed.

5.Results & Conclusion

  • Summarize your results.
  • Note whether your hypothesis was supported.

6.Sign & Date

  • Sign and date your entries at the end.

Tips

  • Always write in pen, never pencil.
  • Avoid leaving blank pages.
  • Be consistent with formatting and headings

👉 Preparing a bone remodeling presentation 

— 
title: “How to Prepare a Bone Remodeling Presentation” 
author: “Your Name” 
format: html 
— 
 
## Introduction 
 
This tutorial explains how to create a clear and effective biology presentation using the topic of bone remodeling. It is designed for students in BIOL 109. 
 
— 
 
## Step 1: Understand the Topic 
 
Before creating slides, research the key concepts: 
 
- Bone remodeling is the process of bone renewal 
- It involves osteoclasts (break down bone) and osteoblasts (build bone) 
- It maintains strength and calcium balance 
 
— 
 
## Step 2: Plan Your Slides 
 
Organize your presentation into sections: 
 
1. Introduction   
2. Bone cells   
3. Remodeling process   
4. Regulation   
5. Clinical relevance   
6. Summary   
 
— 
 
## Step 3: Create Clear Slides 
 
Each slide should: 
 
- Have a clear title   
- Use bullet points (not paragraphs)   
- Include labeled diagrams   
 
Example: 
 
**Slide: Bone Cells** 
- Osteoclasts → bone resorption   
- Osteoblasts → bone formation   
- Osteocytes → signaling   
 
— 
 
## Step 4: Use Visuals Effectively 
 
Include: 
 
- Diagrams of bone cells   
- Step-by-step remodeling cycle   
- Comparison images (healthy vs diseased bone)   
 
Avoid clutter and keep visuals simple. 
 
— 
 
## Step 5: Practice Delivery 
 
- Speak clearly and do not read slides   
- Explain diagrams in your own words   
- Keep timing consistent (1–2 minutes per slide)   
 
— 
 
## Step 6: Cite Your Sources 
 
Always include references. Example: 
 
## References 
 
- OpenStax. *Anatomy & Physiology*.   
- National Institutes of Health (NIH) – Bone Health   
 

 

✅ Step 5: Add Citations (Quarto Requirement)
